Getting started

  1. Mine Arcane Ore, found in stone and deepslate between Y −48 and 48. It drops Arcane Dust, which is affected by Fortune.
  2. Craft a Spellbook and an Apprentice Wand.
  3. Build a Tier 1 shrine (see below) and craft a few Spell Scrolls.
  4. Put a scroll on the pedestal and right-click the floating crystal to run the ritual. The spell is written into your book.
  5. Open the book, click the spell to make it active, then right-click with your wand to cast.

The Void Crystal Shrine

The shrine is a multiblock that grows. Each tier adds more blocks around and above the last one. As you upgrade, the floating crystal gets bigger and more spells unlock. Hold a wand near the crystal to see miniature ghost blocks wherever the next tier still needs a block.

Learning spells (rituals)

  1. Right-click the Arcane Pedestal with a spell scroll to place it there. Right-click again with an empty hand to take it back.
  2. Right-click the Void Crystal Core. If the shrine tier is high enough for that spell, the ritual starts.
  3. Stay within 10 blocks for 5 seconds. A beam rises from the pedestal, the runes light up one by one, and the crystal spins faster.
  4. When the ritual finishes, the scroll is consumed and the spell is written into your spellbook.

The ritual breaks if you take the scroll off the pedestal or break part of the shrine. It also fizzles if you walk too far away. In both cases you keep the scroll.

Casting

Spellbook

Right-click it or press B. There is one tab per school. Click a learned spell to make it your active spell. The book must be somewhere in your inventory for you to cast.

Wand

Right-click to cast the active spell. V moves to the next spell, and sneak + mouse wheel cycles spells while you hold a wand. You can bind a key for "Previous Spell" in Controls.

Mana

Your mana bar sits just right of the hotbar, with the active spell's icon and cooldown beside it. Everyone starts with 100 max mana.

SourceHow
Passive regen1 mana per second, all the time
Shrine circleStand inside an active shrine's rune circle for 3× regen (more at higher tiers)
MoonlightOutdoors at night: +25% regen. During a full moon: +75%
Mana PotionsMinor (30), normal (60), Greater (120). Brewed in a brewing stand, see below
Mana GemStores 200 mana, and right-click draws it into you. Recharge it by placing it on an active shrine's pedestal (10 per second per tier)
Mana CrystalPermanently adds +20 max mana. You can use 3 per shrine tier you have attuned to

You attune to a shrine by standing in its circle or by activating its crystal. Your attunement is the highest shrine tier you have used.
