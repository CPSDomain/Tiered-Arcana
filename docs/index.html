Getting started

  1. Mine Arcane Ore, found in stone and deepslate between Y −48 and 48. It drops Arcane Dust, which is affected by Fortune.
  2. Craft a Spellbook and an Apprentice Wand.
  3. Build a Tier 1 shrine (see below) and craft a few Spell Scrolls.
  4. Put a scroll on the pedestal and right-click the floating crystal to run the ritual. The spell is written into your book.
  5. Open the book, click the spell to make it active, then right-click with your wand to cast.

The Void Crystal Shrine

The shrine is a multiblock that grows. Each tier adds more blocks around and above the last one. As you upgrade, the floating crystal gets bigger and more spells unlock. Hold a wand near the crystal to see miniature ghost blocks wherever the next tier still needs a block.

The four pylons stand on the inner corners of the new base ring, 3 blocks out from the centre in both directions. Tier 2 adds 32 runestones to the outer border, 24 base blocks, plus 8 runestones and 4 Catalyst Crystals for the pylons.

  1. Build the extra rings and the four pylons. Hold a wand near the crystal and ghost blocks show you every missing piece.
  2. When everything is placed, you get "Tier 2 is built!" and an Infused Gem floats over the pedestal as a hint.
  3. Put an Infused Gem on the pedestal and right-click the crystal. The gem is consumed and the shrine awakens to Tier 2. The crystal grows and the regen circle widens to the full 9×9 floor.

Where they go

The two obelisks stand on the outer rune border, one on each side of the crystal (east and west). Each one is 3 blocks wide, centred on the border's middle block, and faces in towards the crystal.

Per obelisk: 11 base blocks, 2 runestones, 2 Blocks of Void Crystal. That's 8 Void Crystals per obelisk. Hold a wand to see the ghost blocks.

  1. Build both obelisks. When they're done you get "Tier 3 is built!" and a Void Crystal floats over the pedestal.
  2. Put a Void Crystal on the pedestal and right-click the crystal to awaken Tier 3.
  3. Pink energy beams start flowing from the tops of the obelisks into the crystal. The crystal grows again, Mana Gems charge 3× as fast, and you can absorb up to 9 Mana Crystals.

Learning spells (rituals)

  1. Right-click the Arcane Pedestal with a spell scroll to place it there. Right-click again with an empty hand to take it back.
  2. Right-click the Void Crystal Core. If the shrine tier is high enough for that spell, the ritual starts.
  3. Stay within 10 blocks for 5 seconds. A beam rises from the pedestal, the runes light up one by one, and the crystal spins faster.
  4. When the ritual finishes, the scroll is consumed and the spell is written into your spellbook.
  5. Tier 2+ spells also need catalysts: put one item on each of the 4 Catalyst Crystals before you start, in any order. Pink streams flow from the crystals into the core, and the catalysts are used up when the ritual finishes. The scroll's tooltip and the spellbook both list the catalysts.

The ritual breaks if you take the scroll off the pedestal or break part of the shrine. It also fizzles if you walk too far away. In both cases you keep the scroll.

Casting

Spellbook

Right-click it or press B. There is one tab per school. Click a learned spell to make it your active spell. The book must be somewhere in your inventory for you to cast.

Wand

Right-click to cast the active spell. V moves to the next spell, and sneak + mouse wheel cycles spells while you hold a wand. You can bind a key for "Previous Spell" in Controls.

JEI & autocrafting

Every Tiered Arcana item is made with a normal crafting-table recipe that has no hidden NBT. So:

  • JEI shows every recipe, and the + button moves the ingredients into a crafting grid or a pattern encoder.
  • Every item can be encoded into a crafting pattern and autocrafted by Tiered Networks or Tiered Industries.
  • Each spell scroll is its own item (tieredarcana:scroll_fireball and so on), so patterns never mix scrolls up.
  • Forge tags are included for other mods' machines: forge:ores/arcane, forge:dusts/arcane, forge:gems/infused, forge:gems/void, forge:storage_blocks/void_crystal.

Two things can't be autocrafted: learning a spell and awakening a shrine tier. Both happen at the shrine, by design.
